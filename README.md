# Comm-Det

## Game Title and Developer
**Comm-Det: Communication Detective**  
**Developer:** Rainers Ļebedevs, rl23031

## Game Concept
*Comm-Det* is a UI-based Roblox detective game where players solve communication problems by identifying unclear, unnecessary or contextually inappropriate English-influenced Latvian and choosing clearer wording for different audiences. 

## Story / Theme
The player is a communication detective investigating misunderstandings in schools, group chats, local communities and other scenarios. Each case involves confusing messages, interviews, and a final corrected announcement.

## Social Good Theme
The game addresses the decline of confident Latvian communication among young people, including frequent mixing of English and Latvian.

It is connected to:
- **SDG 4 – Quality Education:** improves vocabulary, comprehension, and self-expression.
- **SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities:** supports students who find it difficult to communicate clearly.
- **SDG 11 – Sustainable Cities and Communities:** encourages active use of Latvian and better communication between generations.

The game can benefit students, teachers, families, and Latvian-speaking communities.

## Social Good Integration into Gameplay
Players directly practice communication by:
- finding unclear phrases,
- choosing better Latvian alternatives,
- adapting language to different audiences,
- solving misunderstandings through dialogue choices,
- creating a clear final message from predefined text blocks.

## Example Case: “The Missing Workshop”
A school workshop has very few participants because the announcement was unclear:

> “Workshop tmrw after lessons @ lab, bring ur stuff, maybe 15:00ish.”

The player identifies the problems and chooses the clearest correction:

> “Robotikas darbnīca notiks rīt plkst. 15.00 4. laboratorijā. Lūdzu, paņemiet līdzi savus materiālus.”

The player then receives a score for accuracy, clarity, and audience appropriateness.

## Intended Impact
Players should understand that clear, context-appropriate Latvian helps people avoid misunderstandings and communicate with greater confidence. Progress can be measured through case scores and improved choices in later missions.

## Gameplay and Rules
- Single-player
- 8–12 minutes per case
- UI-only Roblox experience
- Main actions: inspect evidence, interview characters, choose corrections, and submit a final report
- Mistakes provide feedback instead of ending the game

## Objectives and Progression
Players solve cases and earn points based on:
- meaning accuracy,
- Latvian clarity,
- appropriate tone.

Successful players progress from **Cadet** to **Senior Communication Detective**.

## Platform and Development Tools
- Roblox Studio
- Luau
- Roblox UI objects such as `ScreenGui`, `Frames`, and `TextButtons`
- GitHub for documentation, scripts, and version control

## Originality
Although language-learning and detective games already exist, *Comm-Det* combines both genres and focuses specifically on Latvian youth communication. Its main difference is that language choices affect the outcome of realistic social cases.

## Scope
The project is designed as a solo-developed UI-based Roblox game. The final version will include one complete and polished communication case, with reusable UI systems that can support additional cases in the future.
